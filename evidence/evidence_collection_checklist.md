# Evidence Collection Checklist

This checklist provides the evidence that should be collected and preserved during the investigation of a phishing-based account compromise.

## Evidence to Collect

- [ ] Phishing email
- [ ] Email headers
- [ ] Suspicious links or attachments
- [ ] Authentication logs
- [ ] Account activity logs
- [ ] Login time and details
- [ ] Device information
- [ ] Security alerts
- [ ] Relevant screenshots

## Evidence Analysis

The collected evidence should be examined to determine:

- The manner in which the phishing attack occurred.
- Whether the user entered their credentials.
- The time when unauthorized access occurred.
- The login attempts associated with the compromised account.
- The actions performed using the affected account.
- Whether other accounts or systems were affected.

## Evidence Preservation

- [ ] Preserve the original phishing email.
- [ ] Preserve relevant email headers.
- [ ] Save relevant logs for further analysis.
- [ ] Record the time and source of collected evidence.
- [ ] Keep screenshots of relevant findings.
- [ ] Prevent unauthorized modification of collected evidence.

## Evidence Handling

All collected evidence should be handled carefully and should only be accessed by authorized personnel involved in the incident investigation.

The collected evidence should be used to support incident analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review.
